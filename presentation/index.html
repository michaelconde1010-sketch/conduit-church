Built for Sunday

Everything your media desk needs

Conduit handles the technical side so your team can focus on worship, not typing.

📄
Sermon notes import

Drop in a PDF of the pastor's notes. Every reference is extracted and the full text pre-loaded, ready to put up in one click. The file never leaves your computer.

🎙
Natural speech detection

Catches verses spoken the way people actually speak — "turn to John chapter three verse sixteen" — and recovers when the microphone mishears a book name.

✦
Paraphrase detection

When the reference is never said aloud, AI reads the transcript and suggests the verse. "The eagle verse" becomes Isaiah 40:31.

🎨
Your church's look

Set the typeface, size, colour, alignment, and background — solid, gradient, or your own imagery. Save it as a preset and it's there next Sunday.

▬
Lower third or full screen

Switch between a bar across the bottom of your camera feed and a full-screen slide, mid-service, without touching the theme.

💬
Messages and countdowns

Put a notice on screen over whatever's showing, or run a countdown before the service starts. Save the ones you use every week.
